"The Gun That Won the West"

Permanent exhibit · Hall 3 · Open daily 10:00–18:00

In the 1870s a new lever-action rifle became so common on the American frontier that newspapers called it "the gun that won the West". Our exhibit tells the story of the people who carried it: ranchers, railway workers, and families moving west in covered wagons.

The exhibit also explains how the telegraph, the railway and the plough changed frontier life as much as any gun did. School groups are welcome, and guided tours run every Saturday morning.

Tickets cost 8 euros, with free entry for visitors over 65 on the first Sunday of every month.
